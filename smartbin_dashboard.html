System Flow
How It Works
End-to-end IoT pipeline from camera to sorted compartment
01 — SCAN
Camera Captures
ESP32-CAM or Python script takes a photo of the waste placed on the scale tray.
02 — CLASSIFY
AI Identifies Type
ML model classifies into Paper, Plastic, Aluminum, or Unknown. Writes to /current/type in Firebase.
03 — WEIGH
HX711 Reads Mass
Load cell reads weight continuously. OLED updates live. Button press confirms.
04 — SORT
Servos Route Waste
PCA9685 drives 4 servos in sequence to direct the item to the correct compartment.
